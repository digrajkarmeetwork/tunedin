Tuned In Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

The short version: Tuned In has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. We don't sell or share your data. Online games send only what's needed to play, and nothing is saved after the game.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, team names, the card packs you write and any game in progress are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What happens when you play online

When you create or join a room, your phone connects to our real-time game service, which runs on Supabase. So the phones in your room can play together, the game sends:

  • the nickname you type
  • a random ID for your phone, created for that game session
  • game moves: clues, dial positions, guesses and scores

These messages go only to the players in your room. They are relayed live and not written to a database. Like any internet service, our provider processes your IP address to deliver the connection and may keep short-lived technical logs for security and reliability.

Anyone who knows your 4-letter room code can join your room, so share it only with people you want to play with, and don't put personal information in nicknames or clues.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, email or phone number
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos
  • No advertising or third-party analytics SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

Children

Tuned In is a general-audience party game. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game doesn't ask for any.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
